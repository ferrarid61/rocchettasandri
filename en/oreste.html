The characters

Oreste called Gambalaria

Told by Bruno Pellegrini

Oreste, a typical character from Trentino, known by everyone, very very loved, a bit particular and so among other things he had this fixation here.

The cow goes to get married

Oreste leaves Campolino, he has to take the cow to the bull. So he prepares her, gives her eyebrows, lipstick on her mouth and then makes her a big crown of flowers, roses and flowers to take her to the bull.

So Oreste passes by my father here who was called Caino. And Oreste, where are you going? "I have to take the cow to get married". And then after Oreste for you go up to the Tosetti house. The Tosetti house takes it up. It stays up half a day practically and comes back and passes again by dad, by Caino, and says: "But I had given it my daughter's perfume behind the bull got scared and didn't want to do anything. Now I'll try again to take it up", practically, that's it.
